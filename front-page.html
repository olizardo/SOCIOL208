📚 Modular Resources & Research Hubs

Comprehensive bibliographies, reference works, textbooks, data repositories, professional societies, and specialist journals.

Reference Works and Further Reading

Methods Books

  • Network Analysis: Methodological Foundations
    An advanced network methods book with a focus on graph-theoretic, algebraic, and algorithmic aspects.
  • Algorithms and Models for Network Data and Link Analysis
    An advanced methods book focusing on algorithmic and model-based aspects of network analysis from a data mining, information science, and computer science perspective.
  • Social Network Data Analytics
    Focusing on algorithmic and model-based aspects of network analysis from a data mining and computer science perspective.
  • Inferential Network Analysis • Book R package
    Introduction to statistical models for social networks with a focus on exponential random graph models (ERGMs).

Handbooks

  • The SAGE Handbook of Social Network Analysis (2nd Edition, 2023)
    Most recent major handbook summarizing the field (UC library account required for access).
  • The Oxford Handbook of Social Networks (2021)
    Major handbook summarizing the field across sociological and methodological domains (UC library access required).
  • The SAGE Handbook of Social Network Analysis (1st Edition, 2011)
    Original major handbook summarizing the field (UC library access required).

Overviews

  • Networks (2nd Edition) – Mark Newman
    A compendium of network concepts and techniques from the perspective of Network Science, written by one of the central scholars in the field.
  • Network Science – Albert-László Barabási
    Open-access interactive textbook on Network Science written by a pioneer in the field.

Theory

  • The Development of Social Network Analysis – Linton C. Freeman
    An accessible, sociologically-informed intellectual and institutional history of the field written by one of the founders.
  • Understanding Social Networks: Theories, Concepts, and Findings – Charles Kadushin
    A non-technical introduction to the core ideas and theories in the field, written by an early adopter of the perspective.

SNA Textbooks and Online Courses

  • Analyzing Social Networks Using R (Borgatti, Everett, Johnson & Agneessens) • Textbook Resources
  • Social Network Analysis: A Guide Using Principles of the ADAPT Model • Book Website
  • Network Analysis: Integrating Social Network Theory, Method, and Application with R (Douglas A. Luke) • Book Website
  • Handbook of Graphs and Networks in People Analytics (With Examples in R and Python) (Keith McNulty) • Book Website
  • R for Social Network Analysis • Book Website (David Schoch)
  • Egocentric Network Analysis Using R • Book Website (Raffaele Vacca)
  • Mark Hoffman’s Course on Methods for Network Analysis (Fall 2021) (Columbia University)

Network Data Repositories

  • Network Repository • Interactive network science data repository with built-in visualization capabilities.
  • Social Network Data Repository • Specialized social network subset with interactive graph analytics.
  • Stanford Large Network Dataset Collection (SNAP) • Curated large-scale network data maintained at Stanford University.
  • UCINET Data Sets • Classic benchmark datasets associated with the network software UCINET.
  • Data Repositories Maintained by Specific Scholars:
    • Tore Opsahl – Longitudinal, weighted, and three-mode social networks.
    • Mark Newman – Classic networks compiled in GML format.

Organizations & Professional Associations

  • International Network for Social Network Analysis (INSNA)
    The leading professional association for researchers interested in social network analysis.
  • ASA Section on Mathematical Sociology
    Official website of the American Sociological Association’s Section for Mathematical Sociology.
  • ASA Section on Decision-Making, Social Networks, and Society
    Official website of the ASA Section for Decision-Making, Social Networks, and Society (formerly Rationality & Society).
  • The Network Science Society
    Leading professional association for researchers across physical, biological, social, mathematical, and computational network sciences.
  • Historical Network Research (HNR)
    International platform for scholars who apply social network analysis to historical data.

Specialist Network Journals

  • Social Networks (Elsevier)
    Top specialist venue for the latest theory, research, and methodological developments in social network analysis.
  • Network Science (Cambridge University Press)
    Interdisciplinary journal dedicated to the formal, computational, and empirical study of complex networks.
  • Connections (INSNA)
    Official peer-reviewed bulletin journal of the International Network for Social Network Analysis.
  • Journal of Social Structure (JoSS)
    Online journal of INSNA publishing interdisciplinary research on the structure of human social life.
  • The Journal of Historical Network Research (JHNR)
    Dedicated to the study of networks from a specifically historical and archival perspective.

Additional Curated Resources

  • Social Network Analysis (SNA) Hub
    A resource hub curated by members of the Social Network Analysis in Scotland (SNAS) Group at the University of Edinburgh.
  • Awesome Network Analysis • Curated list by François Briatte of software packages, tools, datasets, and tutorials for network analysis.
  • Visualizing Historical Networks • Harvard Center for History and Economics project mapping historical interactions and spatial networks.
