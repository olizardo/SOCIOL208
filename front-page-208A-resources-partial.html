📚 Reference Works & Further Reading

Methods Books

  • Network Analysis: Methodological Foundations
    • An advanced network methods book with a focus on graph-theoretic, algebraic, and algorithmic aspects
  • Algorithms and Models for Network Data and Link Analysis
    • An advanced methods book focusing on algorithmic and model-based aspects of network analysis from a data mining, information science, and computer science perspective.
  • Social Network Data Analytics
    • Another advanced methods book focusing on algorithmic and model-based aspects of network analysis from a data mining, information science, and computer science perspective.
  • Inferential Network Analysis
    • Good introduction to statistical models for social networks with a focus on exponential random graph models (ERGMs).
    • Book R package

Handbooks

  • The SAGE Handbook of Social Network Analysis
    • Original (2011) major handbook summarizing the field (UC library account required for access).
  • The SAGE Handbook of Social Network Analysis (2nd Edition)
    • Most recent (2023) major handbook summarizing the field (UC library account required for access).
  • The Oxford Handbook of Social Networks
    • The second-most-recent (2021) major handbook summarizing the field (UC library account required for access).

Overviews 

  • Networks (2nd Edition)
    • A compendium of network concepts and techniques from the perspective of Network Science, written by Mark Newman, one of the central scholars in the field.
  • Network Science
    • An open-access textbook on Network Science was written by Albert-László Barabási, a pioneer in the field.   

Theory   

  • The Development of Social Network Analysis
    • An accessible, sociologically-informed intellectual and institutional history of the field written by one of the founders.
  • Understanding Social Networks: Theories, Concepts, and Findings
    • A non-technical introduction to the core ideas and theories in the field, written by an early adopter of the perspective.

🏛️ Organizations

  • International Network for Social Network Analysis (INSNA)
    • Leading professional association for researchers interested in social network analysis.
  • ASA Section on Mathematical Sociology
    • Official website of the American Sociological Association’s Section for Mathematical Sociology
  • ASA Section on Decision-Making, Social Networks, and Society
    • Official website of the American Sociological Association’s Section for Decision-Making, Social Networks, and Society Section (formerly Rationality & Society)
  • The Network Science Society
    • Leading professional association for researchers interested in network science across the physical, biological, social, mathematical, and computational sciences.
  • Historical Network Research
    • A platform for scholars who study historical networks.

📰 Network Journals

  • Connections (Journal)

  • Journal of Social Structure
    • Online journal of INSNA, publishing interdisciplinary research on the area of social structure.
  • Social Networks (Journal)
    • Top specialist venue for the latest theory, research, and methodological developments in social network analysis.

🧭 Resources

  • Social Network Analysis (SNA) Hub
    • A resource hub is dedicated to all things Social Network Analysis (SNA), curated and maintained by members of the Social Network Analysis in Scotland (SNAS) Group at the University of Edinburgh’s School of Social and Political Science. 
  • Awesome Network Analysis
    • An excellent list of resources to construct, analyze, and visualize network data.
  • Visualizing Historical Networks
    • Website for a network visualization project devoted to mapping “the way people in the past interacted with each other and their surroundings.”
